From Kagoshima to
New Delhi.

We source our tea leaves directly from the historic, mist-covered tea fields of Kagoshima, Japan, a region renowned for producing the world's most vibrant and complex matcha for over 800 years. Our founders traveled to Kyushu to build direct relationships with multi-generational tea farmers, ensuring only the highest ceremonial grade leaves make it to our New Delhi flagship.

Our Philosophy: We stand strictly against pre-whisked shortcuts. Commercial cafes let pre-whisked matcha sit in refrigerated pitchers, allowing oxidation to destroy delicate L-theanine and vital antioxidants within minutes. At Matcha House, every single bowl is exclusively freshly whisked to order using an authentic bamboo Chasen, unlocking peak sweetness and an intoxicating emerald foam.

Every bowl we serve is prepared with a deep, uncompromising respect for the traditional Japanese tea ceremony. From the precise temperature of the water to the rhythmic motion of the bamboo chasen, we believe that true luxury lies in the mastery of these subtle details.

The Origin Story

From Kyushu's Misty Hills to New Delhi's Heart

Matcha House Delhi was born from a transformative journey through the secluded volcanic tea hills of Kagoshima, Japan. Traveling across Kyushu, our founder Selina witnessed multi-generational tea farmers dedicating their lives to cultivating shade-grown tencha with reverence, silence, and patience.

Returning to Delhi, she was struck by a glaring gap: the city's cafes were serving pre-whisked, oxidized matcha mixtures laden with artificial syrups. Matcha House was created as an uncompromising antidote — a serene brutalist sanctuary dedicated strictly to pure, single-origin Kagoshima tea whisked fresh to order for every guest.

Our mission is simple yet radical: to honor centuries of Japanese Chado ritual in the capital, proving that mindfulness is not a luxury, but an essential daily anchor.
