From Uji to
New Delhi.

We source our tea leaves directly from the historic, mist-covered tea fields of Uji, Japan, a region renowned for producing the world's most vibrant and complex matcha for over 800 years. Our founders traveled to Kyoto to build direct relationships with multi-generational tea farmers, ensuring only the highest ceremonial grade leaves make it to our New Delhi flagship.

Every bowl we serve is prepared with a deep, uncompromising respect for the traditional Japanese tea ceremony. From the precise temperature of the water to the rhythmic motion of the bamboo chasen, we believe that true luxury lies in the mastery of these subtle details.
